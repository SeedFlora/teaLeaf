================================================================================
REDISTRIBUTION NOTE
================================================================================

CC BY 4.0 permits redistribution with attribution. This project nonetheless does
NOT redistribute the dataset images, for three reasons: the archive is ~1.3 GB;
the authoritative copy is already permanently available under a DOI; and pointing
to the DOI preserves version provenance that a repackaged copy would weaken.

Reproduction instead uses the download instructions in README.md together with the
committed manifests and SHA-256 checksums, which allow any third party to verify
that they obtained byte-identical data.
